Directory Photos: privacy notice

Directory Photos is an internal tool of Hartamas Real Estate (Malaysia) Sdn Bhd. It is used only by staff whose email address has been added by the company's administrator.

What we receive from Google

When you sign in with Google we receive your name and email address. We use them only to check that you are on the staff allow list and to record who uploaded, viewed or saved photos. We do not receive your password or access any other part of your Google account.

What we store

Photos of building tenant directories, the building name, the shoot date, and an activity log (who signed in, uploaded, viewed or saved photos, and when). This is stored in the company's Google Drive and Google Sheets.

Sharing

We do not sell or share this information outside the company.

Removing access

Staff access is removed by the administrator when it is no longer needed. For questions, contact corporatehartamas@gmail.com.
